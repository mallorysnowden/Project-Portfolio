NASA

NASA Langley Research Center

Summer Research Intern · composites for the Ares V shroud · 2009 & 2010

Fabricated and tested carbon fiber composites for large spacecraft structures.

  1. 01 · Characterize

    Evaluated aerospace resins and adhesives from Cytec, Hexcel, ATK, and 3M using rheometry and calorimetry. Characterized viscosity and cure kinetics to identify thermal cycles that promote uniform laminate consolidation and full cure while minimizing resin bleed and cure-induced embrittlement.

  2. 02 · Correlate

    Developed a MATLAB tool that combined calorimetry and rheology data to map viscosity against degree of cure. Gave the lab its first automated method for correlating these measurements across its materials database.

    Load calorimeter run Filter noise, find exotherm peak Integrate heat released to time t ÷ full-cure heat = degree of cure Overlay rheology data at the same point in the cycle Viscosity vs. degree of cure
  3. 03 · Build

    Fabricated aluminum honeycomb sandwich panels with composite prepreg facesheets for structural testing. Vacuum-bagged and processed the assemblies through controlled thermal cure cycles.
